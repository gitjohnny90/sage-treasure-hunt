## How to run the demo indoors (no walking required)

1. Open the app, then add **`#demo`** to the end of the address and hit go:
   `https://gitjohnny90.github.io/sage-treasure-hunt/#demo`
2. A red **DEMO** badge appears. Every scan page now has a "DEMO ⏩ Skip scan"
   link and every story page has a "DEMO ⏩" button that jumps to the end.
3. To turn it off: open the ☰ menu → "Demo mode: ON", or visit `#demo` again.
4. Demo mode is per-phone and hidden — regular families will never see it.

**Tip for the wow moment:** tap a stop → skip the scan → press play and let a few
seconds of real narration play (Chandler's voice) → tap DEMO ⏩ to complete it.
The confetti, paw stamp, and "X of 26" tally do the rest.

## What's new since the board demo

- **Real narration at 24 of 26 stops** — the recordings from the Drive folder,
  compressed and volume-matched. A stop checks off when its story finishes playing.
- **Real story text** — every placeholder transcript was replaced with a faithful
  transcription of the actual narration (also serves deaf/hard-of-hearing visitors).
- **The hunt can actually be finished now** — finishing all six Commons rooms
  credits the Family Commons itself (this was broken before: families would have
  been stuck at 25/26 forever).
- **No more cheating** — the always-visible DEMO buttons are gone for families,
  the prize screen only opens when all 26 stops are really done, and denying
  camera access no longer fakes a successful scan.
- **Works on camp's weak signal** — the app loads instantly from cache after the
  first visit, fonts are bundled (no Google connection needed), and there's a
  "Save Sage's stories for offline" button on the welcome screen — have families
  tap it on the Commons WiFi and the entire hunt works in dead zones.
- **Renames from the recordings:** stop 18 is "The Memorial Garden" (was Grotto),
  stop 20 is "The South Log Lot" (was South Lot).
- **Safety and polish** — "Start over" now asks for confirmation, completed stops
  can be re-opened to hear the story again, pinch-zoom works for grandparents,
  and the whole thing survives a corrupted phone cache without white-screening.
